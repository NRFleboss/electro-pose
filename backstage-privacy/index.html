Inside Music Backstage — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Inside Music Backstage is an internal tool used by the Inside Music team to manage the Instagram accounts of its own labels. It is not available to the public and it does not serve any other business. This page explains what data the tool handles through Meta's APIs and how to ask for its deletion.

Who we are

Data controller: INSIDE MUSIC, a French limited liability company (SARL), 25 rue du Faubourg Poissonnière, 75009 Paris, France. Contact: contact@insidemusic.com.

What data the tool handles

Only for the Instagram professional accounts owned by Inside Music:

  • the posts we publish and their statistics;
  • the comments left under our posts (text, date, username of the author);
  • the direct messages sent to our accounts (text, date, username of the sender) and our replies.

We do not collect any other information about the people who write to us, and we do not build profiles.

Why

So that the members of our team can read and answer comments and messages addressed to our labels from one place. The data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.

Who has access

Only the members of the Inside Music team who are in charge of our social accounts, through a password-protected tool hosted on our own server in the European Union.

How long we keep it

Comments and messages are kept only as long as they are useful to answer them. They remain available on Instagram, where their author can delete them at any time; a comment or message deleted on Instagram is removed from the tool.

Data deletion

To have your comments or messages removed from the tool, write to contact@insidemusic.com with your Instagram username. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also delete your own comments and unsend your own messages directly in Instagram.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL, cnil.fr).
