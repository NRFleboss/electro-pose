Our story

Launched over a decade ago on YouTube, Electro Posé grew into one of the world’s leading electronic music channels, shaping tastes, breaking artists, and building a global community.

Now reaching millions across YouTube and Spotify, it also operates one of the world’s most influential playlist networks on Spotify and other streaming platforms.

  1. 2013

    The channel opens on YouTube.

  2. 2016

    A label is born out of the channel: Inside Records.

  3. Today

    Playlists on Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer and Amazon Music. Electro Posé is where Inside Music began.
